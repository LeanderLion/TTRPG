✦ XANDOR THISTLEDROP III ✦

A Personal Correspondence

Lady Tallis,

I trust you and your companion, M. Two-Knives, have fared well since we last met. We have not yet picked up the trail of Fnar's armour but undoubtedly will. I, of course, shall keep you advised of our progress and see no reason that we should not all be guesting in the City of Artifice by year's end.

I was wondering, however, between then and now, whether you would be willing to grace me with your company?

There is a quaint tourist establishment down the river south of Lakeside where they offer, of all things, water tasting, bathing and accommodation.

Please send your acceptance and any further correspondence care of one Vandrick Shorewalker at Lakeside, a most resourceful fellow.

I remain, as ever, your devoted friend and occasional accomplice in matters both sensible and regrettable.

May fortune favour your path.

Xandor Thistledrop III
Correspondence of X. Thistledrop III
To:
Lady Tallis Welles
