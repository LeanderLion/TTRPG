A Personal Correspondence

To my esteemed companion,

I trust this letter finds you in possession of all your limbs, your coin purse, and at least a modest quantity of common sense.

Consider this a friendly reminder that our present undertaking grows ever more curious. The whispering ruins north of the river continue to occupy my thoughts, and I confess that I would rather face three ogres than one unanswered mystery.

Should fortune permit, I ask that you make all practical haste toward our agreed rendezvous. Bring any maps, notes, relics, or rumours you may have gathered along your travels.

Above all else, trust neither smiling merchants nor silent wizards. Experience has taught me that both tend to become expensive.

Until our paths cross once more,
